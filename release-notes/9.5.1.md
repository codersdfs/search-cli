# What's New in ghfind 9.5.1

Two TUI bugs in the landing menu and the update panel, plus test-isolation work.

### Fixed

**Landing-screen keys no longer leak into the main view**

- Pressing `/` on the landing menu focused the search input on top of it
- `Space`, `t`, `c`, `?`, and `q` all double-fired through the second key
  listener — help opened and instantly closed, quit ran cleanup twice
- The landing handler now claims its keys with `stopPropagation()` via the
  `landingKeysActive` / `landingConsumesKey` seam, and the global handler
  ignores everything while the landing menu owns the screen
- Also fixed while routing keys: `b` on the landing screen opens bookmarks
  (hint bar updated), and overlays opened from landing hide the menu instead
  of drawing on top of it

**Update panel no longer clips the changelog**

- The modal was hard-capped at 16 rows with the release notes in a single
  unsized text block, so anything past the first ~10 lines was invisible
- The notes area is now a scrollable region (same mechanics as the
  README/org viewers): `↑↓`/`j`/`k` move a fifth of a page,
  `PageUp`/`PageDown` and `Home`/`End` jump, and a scrollbar appears when
  content overflows
- The panel is taller (24 rows), scroll position resets each time it opens,
  and notes render at the panel's real width so wrapped lines don't clip
  horizontally on narrow terminals

### Internal

- Seven test suites built temp dirs from a `Date.now()` stamp
  (`tmpdir()/<name>-<ms>`), a name two concurrent `bun test` processes can
  collide on. They now use `mkdtempSync`, and the login
  `readGhCliToken` / `persistToken` dirs are cleaned up after use
